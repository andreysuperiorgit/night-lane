NIGHT LANE

The same goal, word for word, given to two tools. Both builds are playable on a phone and on desktop.

The goal both gotBuild a one-page browser game called NIGHT LANE. A car drives up an endless road at night, seen from above. Tap the left or right side of the screen, or use the arrow keys, to change lanes and dodge the traffic. Traffic gets faster over time. Show the distance in meters, the best distance, and a crash screen with a restart. It must look great and work on a phone and on desktop. One index.html, no libraries. Open a pull request.
